02 — About Me

A Life Dedicated to the Qur'an

I am Md Tariqul Islam, a Bangladeshi Hafiz of the Holy Qur'an, Qur'anic reciter, Qari and Qur'an educator.

My journey began with the memorization of the Holy Qur'an and gradually developed through Qur'anic recitation, Tajweed, competitions, Tarawih leadership, teaching and Qur'anic education.

Over the years, I have had the opportunity to represent Bangladesh in international Qur'an competitions and participate in Qur'an-related programs and events in different countries.

My international achievements include 1st Place in the Full Qur'an category at the Dubai International Holy Qur'an Award in 2017, 4th Place in its Melody category, and 9th Place at the Kuwait International Qur'an Recitation Competition in 2018.

Beyond competition, my focus has increasingly turned toward advanced Qur'anic studies, Qira'at, authentic Qur'anic transmission and teaching.

I believe that a journey with the Qur'an is not measured only by achievements, but by continuous learning, practice, teaching and service.

My Mission

To serve the Qur'an through accurate recitation, authentic learning, Qira'at, effective teaching and Qur'anic education.
