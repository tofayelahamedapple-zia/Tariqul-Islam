Drop gallery photographs in this folder.

1. Copy the image here, e.g. dubai-2017-first-place.jpg
2. Open  public/data/gallery.json
3. Set the matching entry's "src" to  "assets/gallery/dubai-2017-first-place.jpg"

Recommended: JPG or WebP, ~1600px on the long edge, under 400 KB each.
Categories in use: awards, studies, imam, competitions, teaching, events
